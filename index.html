Our Mission

Rare, but devastating.

A wrong-way driver is one of the most dangerous things you can meet on a highway — almost always head-on, at combined speed, with little time to react. Our mission is simple: stop those encounters before they start.

📱

Technology

Our technology watches the road with you and warns drivers the moment a wrong-way risk is detected — turning seconds into lives saved.

📣

Awareness

We educate drivers and communities on how wrong-way crashes happen and the simple habits that prevent them.

🤝

Community

Every download strengthens the network. The more drivers on board, the safer our roads become for everyone.

Our Technology

Safety tech, being rebuilt.

NMWWD was built around a mobile app — "Keep Our Streets Safe" — that warned drivers of wrong-way risk in real time. We're rebuilding that technology from the ground up to make it better and bring it back to more drivers.

Want to know when it's live again, or help build it? We'd love to hear from you.
